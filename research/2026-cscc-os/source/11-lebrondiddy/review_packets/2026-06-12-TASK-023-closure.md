Mechanism class: still a survivability failure, most consistent with (b) or a
related executor-drain failure: the timer watchdog marks the parent shell task
dead while it is blocked in LoongArch syscall 260 (`wait4`), but the cooperative
executor does not resume `initproc` far enough to print the group timeout END
marker after hackbench's children are killed.

This is no longer a missing timer issue: timer-side per-task watchdog output
continues to fire during the run. It is also not simply missing harness
detection: the harness reliably fails the run after 120s of silence.
